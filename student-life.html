Student Life & Co-Curriculars

Education extends far beyond textbooks. We build character, sportsmanship, and leadership through active student clubs, athletics, and cultural competitions.

Student Clubs & Societies

Every Friday afternoon is dedicated to passionate student-led co-curricular activities.

Debating & Declamation Society

Parliamentary debate styles, Model UN simulations, and Urdu declamation competitions developing razor-sharp rhetoric and poise.

Robotics & Innovators Club

Hands-on construction of motorized models, sensor line-follower vehicles, and preparation for national science olympiads.

Quranic Recitation & Qirat Circle

Mastering classical maqamat of Tajweed recitation under the personal tutelage of certified international Qaris.

Islamic Calligraphy & Visual Arts

Exploring traditional Thuluth and Nastaliq scripts alongside modern acrylic painting, sketch craft, and pottery.

Athletics, Cricket & Table Tennis

Physical conditioning, structured inter-house cricket tournaments, badminton leagues, and martial arts taekwondo sessions.

Community Outreach & Red Crescent

Youth volunteer drives, tree plantation around historic downtown Karachi, first-aid training, and local welfare visits.
